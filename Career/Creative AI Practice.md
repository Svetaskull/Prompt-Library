# Creative AI Practice

## Visual Diary

I use generative AI not only for commercial content production, but also as a tool for personal creative expression.

I maintain a visual diary through Threads, using AI-generated imagery to explore:

- Mood
- Emotions
- Personal associations
- Visual storytelling
- Surreal and conceptual ideas
- Personal aesthetics

---

## Purpose

The visual diary is an independent creative practice.

Unlike commercial content, it is not constrained by:

- Brand guidelines
- Product requirements
- Marketing objectives
- Commercial messaging

This allows experimentation with visual language, atmosphere and narrative.

---

## AI Workflow

The creative process may include:

1. Developing a visual concept
2. Translating an abstract idea into a visual direction
3. Generating initial variations
4. Selecting the strongest result
5. Iterating on composition and details
6. Refining the visual style
7. Publishing the final work

---

## What This Practice Develops

- Prompt development
- Visual direction
- Concept development
- Iterative image generation
- AI-assisted creative workflows
- Visual storytelling
- Personal visual language

---

## Commercial vs Personal AI Work

### Commercial

Goal-driven.

Examples:

- Social media content
- Product photography
- Advertising concepts
- Brand visuals

### Personal

Exploratory.

Examples:

- Visual diary
- Emotional concepts
- Experimental imagery
- Personal narratives

The same AI tools can support both workflows, but the creative constraints and goals are different.

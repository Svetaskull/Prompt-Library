# Interview Questions

Collection of questions relevant to Team Lead, Content Operations and AI-related roles.

---

# Leadership

## Team Management

- How do you organize the work of a team?
- How do you distribute tasks?
- How do you monitor whether tasks are completed?
- How do you deal with an employee who repeatedly misses deadlines?
- How do you onboard a new team member?
- How do you give negative feedback?
- How do you resolve conflicts within a team?

---

## Delegation

- What do you delegate?
- What do you prefer to keep under your control?
- How do you know whether a task was delegated successfully?
- How do you avoid micromanagement?

---

# Operations

## Process Improvement

- How do you identify a broken process?
- How do you decide what should be automated?
- How do you reduce repetitive work?
- How do you document recurring processes?
- How do you measure whether a process improvement actually worked?

---

## Problem Solving

- Tell me about a recurring problem you had to solve.
- How do you investigate a problem when the cause is unclear?
- How do you prioritize several problems at once?
- What do you do when your first solution doesn't work?

---

# Content

## Content Strategy

- How do you choose content topics?
- How do you evaluate whether an idea is worth producing?
- What makes a strong hook?
- How do you determine whether content is successful?
- How do you balance creativity and business goals?

---

## Content Operations

- How do you organize high-volume content production?
- How do you maintain quality when content volume increases?
- How do you coordinate multiple people involved in content production?
- How do you handle missed publications or production delays?

---

# AI

## AI Workflows

- How do you use AI in your daily work?
- Which tasks should be automated with AI?
- Which tasks should remain under human control?
- How do you evaluate AI-generated content?
- How do you improve AI outputs?
- How do you build reusable AI workflows?

# Behavioral Questions

## Common Questions

- Tell me about yourself.
- Why are you looking for a new opportunity?
- What are your strongest professional skills?
- What is an area you are currently improving?
- Tell me about a difficult professional situation.
- Tell me about a mistake you made.
- Tell me about a successful project.
- Tell me about a conflict you resolved.
- Tell me about a time you had to make a decision with incomplete information.

---

# Answer Framework

For experience-based questions, structure the answer as:

1. Situation
2. Problem
3. Action
4. Result
5. Lesson

Focus on:

- What I personally did
- Why I made the decision
- What changed afterward
- What I learned

Avoid describing only what the team did.

---

# Questions To Ask The Interviewer

## Role

- What would be the main priorities during the first three months?
- What does success look like in this role?
- What are the biggest challenges the team currently faces?

## Team

- How is the team structured?
- How many people would I manage?
- What are the current levels of the team members?
- How is performance evaluated?

## Processes

- Which processes are already established?
- Where does the team currently experience the most friction?
- How much freedom does the team lead have to change processes?

## Growth

- What does career progression look like for this role?
- How does the company support development of team leads?

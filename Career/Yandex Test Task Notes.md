# Yandex Test Task — Interview Notes

## 1. Goal

Increase conversion from 26% to 31.2%.

31.2% = 26% × 1.20

The target is a 20% relative increase, not +5.2 percentage points.

---

## 2. Why Start With Top-GMV Partners

The goal is not simply to improve the average conversion.

Improving conversion among partners generating the largest GMV can produce a larger business impact.

Priority:

1. High GMV + low conversion
2. Medium GMV + low conversion
3. Lower GMV / testing opportunities

---

## 3. Why Product Cards

The task provides two strong signals:

- Standard structure: 32% conversion
- Non-standard structure: 23%

And:

- Fully completed cards: 33%
- Poorly completed cards: 21%

This makes card structure and completeness strong initial hypotheses.

---

## 4. Team Capacity

Team capacity:

600 positions/day

Current workload:

250 positions/day

Therefore there is available capacity for optimization and additional work.

New products:

2,500/week

The process needs to account for both backlog optimization and incoming inventory.

---

## 5. Main Strategy

Improve conversion through:

- Product card standardization
- Content completeness
- Prioritization by business impact
- Testing and measuring changes
- Continuous work with new products

---

## 6. What I Would Measure

Primary metric:

Conversion rate

Secondary metrics:

- GMV
- Conversion by partner
- Conversion by product category
- Card completeness
- Share of standardized cards
- Productivity of the content team
- Time from identification to correction

---

## 7. If Results Do Not Improve

I would not immediately conclude that the strategy failed.

First:

1. Check data quality.
2. Compare treated vs untreated products.
3. Check whether changes were actually implemented.
4. Segment results by partner/category.
5. Identify which hypothesis failed.
6. Test the next hypothesis.

---

## 8. Team Management

I would avoid assigning the entire team to one large undifferentiated backlog.

Instead:

- Prioritize work by business impact.
- Define clear ownership.
- Set daily/weekly targets.
- Track throughput and quality.
- Review results regularly.
- Reallocate capacity when priorities change.

---

## 9. AI Usage

AI was used as a tool for:

- Structuring the problem
- Generating and comparing approaches
- Checking calculations
- Improving presentation structure

Final prioritization, assumptions and decisions were reviewed manually.

# Interview Stories

Collection of real professional situations that can be used during interviews.

---

## Story Structure

### Situation

What was happening?

### Problem

What needed to be solved?

### Action

What did I personally do?

### Result

What changed as a result?

### Lesson

What did I learn?

---

# Leadership

## Managing Account Managers

### Situation

I became responsible for coordinating account managers and ensuring that publication processes were completed correctly and on time.

### Problem

The role required coordination across multiple accounts, platforms, reports and recurring operational tasks.

### Action

- Organized responsibilities
- Clarified processes
- Monitored reporting
- Identified recurring problems
- Documented workflows
- Created clearer instructions

### Result

The work became more structured and easier to monitor.

### Lesson

Clear processes and responsibilities reduce unnecessary coordination.

---

# Process Improvement

## Turning Repeated Questions Into Documentation

### Situation

Recurring processes generated repeated questions from team members.

### Problem

The same information had to be explained multiple times.

### Action

- Identified recurring questions
- Documented the process
- Created reusable instructions
- Clarified responsibilities

### Result

The information became available as a reusable resource instead of remaining only in conversations.

### Lesson

If a question repeats, the process probably needs documentation.

# Problem Solving

## TikTok Zero Views Investigation

### Situation

Some TikTok accounts were receiving zero views.

### Problem

The cause was unclear and required systematic investigation.

### Action

- Collected possible hypotheses
- Structured the investigation
- Documented findings
- Tracked experiments
- Coordinated with the team

### Result

Created a repeatable investigation framework instead of solving each account problem independently.

### Lesson

Recurring problems should be turned into documented troubleshooting processes.

# AI

## Building An AI Knowledge Base

### Situation

AI tools were increasingly becoming part of everyday content and operational workflows.

### Problem

Useful prompts, workflows and lessons could easily become scattered across different conversations and tools.

### Action

- Created a structured GitHub knowledge base
- Organized prompts and frameworks
- Documented workflows
- Collected lessons learned
- Built reusable resources

### Result

Created a growing professional knowledge system.

### Lesson

AI becomes more useful when individual experiments are turned into reusable workflows.

# Content Strategy

## Changing The Content Approach

### Situation

Different types of educational content produced different levels of audience engagement.

### Problem

Generic ingredient-focused topics were not always as compelling as problem-focused topics.

### Action

Shifted the content approach toward:

- Symptoms
- Recognizable problems
- Seasonal relevance
- Practical takeaways
- Stronger hooks

### Result

Developed a clearer framework for selecting content topics.

### Lesson

Start with the audience's problem rather than the product.

# AI Tools Comparison

Personal observations based on practical use.

---

# ChatGPT

## Best For

- Content creation
- Brainstorming
- Documentation
- SOP creation
- Strategy development

## Strengths

- Fast
- Flexible
- Strong reasoning
- Good for structured work

## Weaknesses

- Requires good prompting
- May produce generic outputs

---

# Nana Banana

## Best For

- Instagram visuals
- Product concepts
- Marketplace content

## Strengths

- Strong photorealism
- Good visual quality
- Useful for social media

## Weaknesses

- Requires clear visual direction

---

# Midjourney

## Best For

- Creative concepts
- Artistic visuals
- Inspiration

## Strengths

- High creativity
- Strong aesthetics

## Weaknesses

- Less practical for product-focused content

---

# Kling

## Best For

- Image animation
- Short-form content

## Strengths

- Easy animation workflow

## Weaknesses

- Depends heavily on source image quality

---

# Sora

## Best For

- Experimental content

## Personal Observation

Results are inconsistent for practical marketplace use.

---

# Key Lesson

The best tool depends on the task.

A good workflow often combines multiple AI tools rather than relying on a single one.

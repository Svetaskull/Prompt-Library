# Account Management Team

## Project Overview

Managing a team responsible for publishing and maintaining social media accounts across multiple platforms.

---

## Role

Lead Account Manager

Responsibilities:

- Team coordination
- Task distribution
- Process control
- Reporting control
- Problem resolution
- Communication with management

## Lessons Learned

### Team Management

- Clear processes reduce mistakes.
- Documentation saves time.
- Standardized workflows improve consistency.

### Content Operations

- Early issue detection is critical.
- Reporting must be simple and transparent.
- Platform-specific problems require different solutions.

# Beautery Product Photography

## Purpose

Generate realistic product images for Instagram and marketplace content.

---

## Requirements

- Photorealistic style
- No text on image
- No infographics
- No before/after collages
- Vertical Instagram format
- Space at the top for logo placement
- Natural lighting
- Premium look

---

## Preferred Details

- Transparent supplement jars
- Realistic labels
- Grey countertop
- Nude manicure
- White or black cardigan sleeves
- Soft depth of field

---

## Negative Prompt

- Cartoon style
- CGI look
- Fake plastic textures
- Empty white packaging
- Text overlays
- Watermarks

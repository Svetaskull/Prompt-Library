# Summer Swelling

## Format

Instagram Carousel

---

## Slide 1

WHY DO YOUR RINGS AND SHOES FEEL TIGHTER IN SUMMER?

It's more common than many people think.

---

## Slide 2

Hot weather affects how the body manages fluids.

---

## Slide 3

Blood vessels may expand in response to heat.

This is part of the body's cooling process.

---

## Slide 4

As a result, some people notice temporary swelling.

Especially in:

- Feet
- Ankles
- Hands

---

## Slide 5

Long periods of sitting or standing may make it more noticeable.

---

## Slide 6

What can help?

- Stay hydrated
- Move regularly
- Avoid prolonged inactivity
- Elevate legs when possible

---

## Slide 7

Mild summer swelling can be a normal response to heat.

But persistent or severe symptoms should be discussed with a healthcare professional.

# Why Summer Workouts Feel Harder

## Format

Instagram Carousel

---

## Slide 1

WHY DO YOUR WORKOUTS FEEL HARDER IN SUMMER?

Even when your training plan hasn't changed.

---

## Slide 2

Your body works harder in hot weather.

It must cool itself while also supporting physical activity.

---

## Slide 3

This means additional energy is spent on temperature regulation.

Not just on exercise.

---

## Slide 4

You may notice:

- Faster fatigue
- Higher perceived effort
- Longer recovery
- Reduced performance

---

## Slide 5

Hydration also becomes more important.

Even mild fluid loss can affect how you feel during training.

---

## Slide 6

What can help?

- Train during cooler hours
- Prioritize hydration
- Allow more recovery
- Adjust expectations during heat waves

---

## Slide 7

Harder workouts don't always mean you're less fit.

Sometimes your body is simply working against the heat.

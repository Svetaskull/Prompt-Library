# Why Legs Cramp More Often In Summer

## Format

Instagram Carousel

---

## Slide 1

WHY DO YOUR LEGS CRAMP MORE OFTEN IN SUMMER?

Many people blame training.

But the reason may be something else.

---

## Slide 2

During hot weather your body loses more fluids through sweat.

And it loses more than just water.

---

## Slide 3

Along with fluids, your body also loses electrolytes.

These minerals help muscles function normally.

---

## Slide 4

When hydration and electrolyte balance are affected, muscles may become more prone to cramping.

Especially during exercise.

---

## Slide 5

The risk may increase when:

- You train in heat
- You sweat heavily
- Recovery is poor
- Hydration is inconsistent

---

## Slide 6

What can help?

- Drink fluids regularly
- Pay attention to recovery
- Consider electrolyte intake during prolonged sweating
- Avoid sudden increases in training load

---

## Slide 7

Summer cramps are not always about training harder.

Sometimes recovery and hydration deserve more attention.

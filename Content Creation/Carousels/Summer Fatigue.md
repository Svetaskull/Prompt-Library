# Summer Fatigue

## Format

Instagram Carousel

---

## Slide 1

SUMMER FATIGUE ISN'T ALWAYS ABOUT LACK OF SLEEP

Feeling tired more often lately?

The heat may be playing a bigger role than you think.

---

## Slide 2

Many people expect to feel more energetic during summer.

But in reality, hot weather can place additional stress on the body.

---

## Slide 3

Your body constantly works to maintain a stable temperature.

That process requires energy.

---

## Slide 4

Hot weather may contribute to:

- Faster fatigue
- Reduced concentration
- Lower motivation
- Feeling drained by the end of the day

---

## Slide 5

Physical activity can make this effect even more noticeable.

Especially during heat waves.

---

## Slide 6

What can help?

- Prioritize hydration
- Get enough sleep
- Schedule demanding activities during cooler hours
- Allow time for recovery

---

## Slide 7

If summer feels more exhausting than expected, you're not imagining it.

Your body may simply be working harder than usual.

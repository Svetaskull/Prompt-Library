# Summer Headaches

## Format

Instagram Carousel

---

## Slide 1

HEADACHES IN SUMMER AREN'T ALWAYS CAUSED BY THE SUN

The real reason may surprise you.

---

## Slide 2

Many people blame bright sunlight immediately.

But several factors can contribute to headaches during hot weather.

---

## Slide 3

Hot weather often changes:

- Hydration habits
- Daily routine
- Physical activity levels
- Sleep quality

---

## Slide 4

Even mild dehydration can affect how you feel.

Including energy levels and overall comfort.

---

## Slide 5

Heat may also increase fatigue and place additional stress on the body.

---

## Slide 6

What can help?

- Drink fluids consistently
- Avoid long periods in extreme heat
- Prioritize sleep
- Pay attention to recovery

---

## Slide 7

Summer headaches may have multiple causes.

Looking beyond the sun is often a good place to start.

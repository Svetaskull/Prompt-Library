Does the audience recognize the problem?

Score: 1-5

## Question 2

Score: 1-5

Is the topic seasonally relevant?

Score: 1-5

Score: 1-5

## Question 5

Does the topic have practical value?

Score: 1-5

## Evaluation

22-25 = Strong Idea

18-21 = Good Idea

14-17 = Average Idea

Below 14 = Skip

# Content Angles

Collection of content angles for educational and marketing content.

---

# Problem

Explain a common problem.

Examples:

- Why do legs cramp in summer?
- Why are you tired after workouts?
- Why does your skin feel dry?

---

# Symptom

Start from a symptom people recognize.

Examples:

- Constant fatigue
- Headaches
- Poor recovery
- Dry skin

---

# Myth

Debunk a common misconception.

Examples:

- More water always solves dehydration.
- Fatigue is always caused by lack of sleep.
- Expensive skincare is always better.

---

# Mistake

Highlight common mistakes.

Examples:

- Summer hydration mistakes
- Skincare mistakes
- Supplement mistakes

---

# Quiz

Turn the topic into a question.

Examples:

- What causes this symptom?
- Can you guess the reason?
- True or false?

---

# Comparison

Compare two approaches.

Examples:

- Electrolytes vs plain water
- Day cream vs night cream
- Whey vs plant protein

---

# Checklist

Provide a quick self-assessment.

Examples:

- 5 signs you may need more recovery
- 7 signs your skin is dehydrated

---

# Seasonal

Connect the topic to a season.

Examples:

- Summer fatigue
- Winter immunity
- Spring allergies

---

# Trend

Connect to current trends.

Examples:

- Biohacking
- Recovery
- Healthy aging

---

# Story

Use a relatable situation.

Examples:

- You drink enough water but still feel tired.
- You sleep 8 hours but wake up exhausted.

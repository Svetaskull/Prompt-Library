# Beautery Content Framework

## Core Principle

People care about problems more than products.

Bad:

- Benefits of Magnesium

Better:

- Why your legs cramp more often in summer

---

## Content Categories

### Symptoms

Examples:

- Fatigue
- Headaches
- Cramps
- Poor recovery
- Low energy

---

### Seasonal Content

Examples:

- Summer fatigue
- Heat and recovery
- Hydration
- Summer workouts

---

### Myths

Examples:

- Magnesium myths
- Vitamin D myths
- Omega-3 myths

---

### Mistakes

Examples:

- Supplement mistakes
- Hydration mistakes
- Recovery mistakes

---

## Preferred Content Formula

Problem
↓
Why it happens
↓
What to do

---

## Hook Formula

Recognition
↓
Curiosity
↓
Explanation

Example:

Why do your workouts feel harder in summer?

---

## Image Formula

One idea
↓
One visual story
↓
No text
↓
Photorealism

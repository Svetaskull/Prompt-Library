# Content Matrix

## Educational

Purpose:
Build trust and expertise.

Examples:

- Why summer fatigue happens
- What electrolytes do
- How collagen works

---

## Problem Solving

Purpose:
Address a specific pain point.

Examples:

- Why your legs cramp
- Why your skin feels dry
- Why recovery feels slower

---

## Myth Busting

Purpose:
Correct misconceptions.

Examples:

- More water isn't always enough
- Fatigue isn't always lack of sleep
- Expensive skincare isn't always better

---

## Engagement

Purpose:
Increase comments and shares.

Examples:

- Quiz
- Poll
- True or false
- Guess the reason

---

## Product Related

Purpose:
Connect education with products.

Examples:

- When electrolytes may be useful
- How magnesium fits into recovery
- What vitamin D does

---

## Seasonal

Purpose:
Use current context.

Examples:

- Summer hydration
- Winter immunity
- Spring recovery

---

## Lifestyle

Purpose:
Make content relatable.

Examples:

- Office workers
- Parents
- Athletes
- Students

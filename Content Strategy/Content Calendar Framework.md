# Content Calendar Framework

## Purpose

A practical framework for maintaining a balanced content mix.

The goal is to avoid publishing too much of the same type of content while maintaining a balance between educational, seasonal, engagement and product-related topics.

The exact mix can be adjusted depending on current priorities, available content and audience response.

---

## Recommended Weekly Mix

### Educational

~2 posts per week

Purpose:

- Explain common problems
- Answer audience questions
- Build trust and expertise

Examples:

- Why it happens
- How it works
- What causes this symptom

---

### Myth Busting

~1 post per week

Purpose:

- Correct common misconceptions
- Create curiosity
- Encourage discussion

Examples:

- Supplement myths
- Hydration myths
- Recovery myths

---

### Seasonal

~1 post per week

Purpose:

- Connect content with current audience situations
- Increase topical relevance

Examples:

- Summer recovery
- Hydration in hot weather
- Seasonal fatigue

---

### Engagement

~1 post per week

Purpose:

- Encourage interaction
- Learn more about audience questions and opinions

Examples:

- Quiz
- Checklist
- Poll
- True or false

---

### Product Related

~1 post per week

Purpose:

- Connect educational content with relevant products
- Explain when a product category may be useful
- Support product discovery without turning content into direct advertising

Examples:

- Magnesium
- Electrolytes
- Protein

---

## Important

Content categories can overlap.

For example:

"Why do you feel more tired during summer workouts?"

can be:

- Educational
- Seasonal
- Problem-oriented

A product-related post can also be educational or seasonal.

The categories are used to maintain balance, not to force every piece of content into a single category.

---

## Adjusting The Mix

The weekly mix should be adjusted when:

- Audience response changes
- Seasonal priorities change
- A specific topic requires more coverage
- Product priorities change
- Content production capacity changes
- Performance data suggests a different content balance

The calendar should support content strategy rather than become a rigid publishing rule.

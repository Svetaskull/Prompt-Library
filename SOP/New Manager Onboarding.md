# New Manager Onboarding

## Goal

Help a new account manager start working quickly and correctly.

---

## Step 1. Access

Provide access to:

- Google Sheets
- Reporting documents
- Communication groups
- Required accounts

---

## Step 2. Documentation

Review:

- Account Manager Regulations
- Publication Rules
- Reporting Rules

---

## Step 3. Accounts

Assign:

- Platforms
- Account groups
- Responsibilities

---

## Step 4. Reporting

Explain:

- Daily reports
- Required metrics
- Deadlines

---

## Step 5. First Week Checklist

- Access received
- Accounts assigned
- First publications completed
- Reporting completed

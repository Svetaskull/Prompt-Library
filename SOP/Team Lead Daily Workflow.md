# Team Lead Daily Workflow

## Purpose

Coordinate and control the work of account managers, ensure that the publication plan is being fulfilled, maintain reporting quality, and identify operational risks in time.

---

## Daily Workflow

### 1. Check Team Status

At the beginning of the working day:

- Check the current status of account managers.
- Identify absences or availability issues.
- Review urgent tasks and known problems.
- Check whether there are accounts requiring additional attention.

---

### 2. Check Publication Plan

Review the current publication status across assigned accounts.

Check:

- Whether publications are happening according to plan.
- Whether there are missed or delayed publications.
- Whether content is available for upcoming publications.
- Whether any account or platform is falling behind.

---

### 3. Monitor Account Managers

During the working day:

- Monitor task completion.
- Check reporting.
- Answer operational questions.
- Help resolve issues that cannot be handled independently.
- Identify recurring problems.

The goal is control without unnecessary micromanagement.

---

### 4. Check Reporting

Review reports for:

- Completeness
- Accuracy
- Timeliness
- Correct account status
- Correct publication information

If errors are found:

1. Identify the issue.
2. Clarify what needs to be corrected.
3. Make sure the correction is completed.
4. Identify whether the issue is recurring.

---

### 5. Identify Risks

Pay attention to:

- Missed publications
- Delayed tasks
- Account problems
- Reporting errors
- Lack of content
- Repeated mistakes
- Platform-specific issues
- Workload problems

Prioritize issues that may affect the overall publication plan.

---

### 6. Problem Escalation

When a problem cannot be resolved at the account-manager level:

1. Collect the relevant information.
2. Determine the impact of the problem.
3. Identify possible solutions.
4. Escalate the issue to the appropriate person.
5. Track the issue until it is resolved.

---

### 7. Process Improvement

When the same problem appears repeatedly:

- Identify the root cause.
- Check whether the existing process is unclear.
- Update documentation if necessary.
- Create an SOP or checklist when appropriate.
- Communicate the updated process to the team.

### Principle

Do not solve the same problem manually over and over again.

Turn recurring problems into processes.

---

### 8. End-of-Day Control

Before the end of the working day:

- Check the overall publication status.
- Review unresolved issues.
- Confirm that important problems have been escalated.
- Check reporting completion.
- Identify risks for the next working day.

---

# Quality Checklist

- [ ] Team status checked
- [ ] Publication plan reviewed
- [ ] Delays and missed publications identified
- [ ] Reporting reviewed
- [ ] Problems identified
- [ ] Important issues escalated
- [ ] Recurring problems documented
- [ ] End-of-day status checked

---

# Management Principle

The Team Lead should not become the person who manually fixes every problem.

The goal is to create a system where:

**account manager → solves routine issue**

**Team Lead → handles exceptions, risks and process improvement**

**documentation → prevents the same problem from appearing repeatedly**

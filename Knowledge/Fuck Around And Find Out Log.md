# Fuck Around And Find Out Log

Real mistakes, failed experiments and lessons learned from practice.

---

## Content

### What Happened

Created content around product benefits without connecting it to a recognizable problem.

### Result

The content was less engaging than problem-focused topics.

### Fuck Around

"Let's tell people how useful magnesium is."

### Find Out

People are more interested in:

"Why do my legs cramp?"

### Lesson

Start with the audience's problem, not the product.

---

## Image Generation

### What Happened

Tried to communicate too many ideas in one image.

### Result

The visual became confusing and overly complicated.

### Fuck Around

"Let's put everything into one picture."

### Find Out

The image loses its main idea.

### Lesson

One image = one visual story.

---

## AI

### What Happened

Used a vague prompt without enough context.

### Result

The output was generic or completely missed the intended direction.

### Fuck Around

"Make it beautiful and professional."

### Find Out

AI has no fucking idea what "beautiful" means in this specific context.

### Lesson

Give the model context, goal, audience and constraints.

---

## Documentation

### What Happened

Useful information was left in chat instead of being documented.

### Result

Had to search through old conversations to find it again.

### Fuck Around

"I'll remember where this was."

### Find Out

No, you fucking won't.

### Lesson

If information is useful, give it a permanent home.

---

## Management

### What Happened

The same process had to be explained multiple times.

### Result

Repeated questions and wasted time.

### Fuck Around

"Да я просто ещё раз объясню."

### Find Out

You will explain it again next week.

### Lesson

If the same question appears repeatedly, create documentation or an SOP.

---

## General Rule

If something goes wrong more than once:

STOP.

Document what happened.

Find the root cause.

Create a better process.

Don't rely on remembering not to fuck up again.

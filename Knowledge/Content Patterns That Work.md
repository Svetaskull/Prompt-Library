# Content Patterns That Work

Collection of recurring content structures that have shown potential or positive results in Beautery content.

These patterns are reusable frameworks, not guarantees of performance.

They should be adapted to the topic, audience and context.

---

# Pattern 1: Symptom First

## Formula

Symptom
↓
Explanation
↓
Solution

## Example

Why do your legs cramp more often in summer?

---

# Pattern 2: Myth Busting

## Formula

Popular belief
↓
Why it is wrong
↓
Correct explanation

## Example

Magnesium should be taken only before sleep.

---

# Pattern 3: Seasonal Relevance

## Formula

Current season
↓
Common problem
↓
Practical advice

## Example

Why workouts feel harder during hot weather.

---

# Pattern 4: Recognition

## Formula

Describe a situation
↓
Audience recognizes themselves
↓
Provide explanation

## Example

You sleep enough but still feel tired.

---

# Pattern 5: Hidden Cause

## Formula

Common problem
↓
Unexpected reason
↓
Solution

## Example

Your headaches may not be caused by stress.

---

# Pattern 6: Mistakes

## Formula

Common mistake
↓
Consequence
↓
Correct approach

## Example

3 mistakes people make when taking supplements.

---

# Pattern 7: Checklist

## Formula

Question
↓
Checklist
↓
Recommendation

## Example

How to know if you are drinking enough water.

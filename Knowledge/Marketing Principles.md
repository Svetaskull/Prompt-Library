# Marketing Principles

## People care about problems, not products

Bad:

- Buy magnesium

Better:

- Why your muscles cramp more often in summer

---

## Specific beats general

Bad:

- Healthy lifestyle tips

Better:

- Why recovery feels harder in hot weather

---

## Symptoms outperform explanations

People recognize symptoms before they recognize causes.

Examples:

- Fatigue
- Headaches
- Cramps
- Dry skin

---

## Curiosity drives clicks

Examples:

- Why does this happen?
- Most people don't know this.
- The reason may surprise you.

---

## Simplicity wins

If it takes effort to understand, engagement drops.

---

## Practical value beats theory

People want:

- What it means
- Why it happens
- What to do

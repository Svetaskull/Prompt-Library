# Content Patterns That Fail

Collection of recurring content patterns that tend to produce weaker content.

These patterns are not absolute rules. Context, audience and execution can affect performance.

---

## Ingredient First

### Problem

Starting with a product or ingredient instead of a recognizable audience problem.

### Bad Example

Benefits of magnesium.

### Better Example

Why your muscles cramp after workouts.

---

## Too Much Theory

### Problem

Trying to explain too much theory without giving the audience a practical takeaway.

### Better Approach

Focus on the information needed to understand the problem and what to do next.

---

## Generic Health Advice

### Problem

Topics are too broad and do not address a specific audience situation.

### Bad Example

10 healthy lifestyle tips.

### Better Approach

Focus on a specific problem, situation or question.

---

## Multiple Topics In One Post

### Problem

Trying to cover several unrelated ideas weakens the main message.

### Better Approach

Give each piece of content one clear main idea.

---

## Complex Language

### Problem

Overly technical or complicated language makes content harder to understand.

### Better Approach

Use clear language and explain technical concepts when they are necessary.

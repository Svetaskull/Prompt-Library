# Content Patterns That Fail

---

## Ingredient First

Problem:

People care more about problems than ingredients.

Bad Example:

Benefits of magnesium.

Better Example:

Why your muscles cramp after workouts.

---

## Too Much Theory

Problem:

People want practical value.

---

## Generic Health Advice

Problem:

Too broad.

Example:

10 healthy lifestyle tips.

---

## Multiple Topics In One Post

Problem:

Weak focus.

---

## Complex Language

Problem:

Lower readability.

# AI Workflow Ideas

## Content Plan Creation

### Goal

Generate a monthly content plan faster.

### Workflow

1. Analyze seasonality.
2. Generate topic ideas.
3. Group by categories.
4. Create publication schedule.
5. Generate drafts.

### Tools

- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Google Sheets

5. Quality check.

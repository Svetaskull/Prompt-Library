# AI Workflow Examples

Collection of AI-assisted workflows used or explored in content production, product content, operations and knowledge management.

These workflows demonstrate how AI can be integrated into repeatable processes rather than used only for one-off tasks.

## Key Principle

AI workflows are most useful when they turn repetitive tasks into repeatable processes.

A typical workflow follows:

Input  
↓  
AI Assistance  
↓  
Human Review  
↓  
Final Output

AI accelerates production, while human review remains responsible for quality, relevance and final decisions.

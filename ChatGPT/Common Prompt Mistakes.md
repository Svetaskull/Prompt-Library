# Common Prompt Mistakes

## Too Vague

Bad:

Write a post about vitamins.

---

## No Audience

Missing target audience information.

---

## No Goal

Missing desired outcome.

---

## Too Many Requests

Trying to solve multiple tasks in one prompt.

---

## No Constraints

Missing important limitations.

---

## Ignoring Iteration

Expecting perfect output from the first attempt.

---

## Focusing On Prompt Length

Longer prompts are not always better.

---

## Missing Examples

Without examples, outputs may be inconsistent.

---

## Personal Observations

### Observation 1

Clear tasks produce better results.

### Observation 2

Specific prompts outperform generic prompts.

### Observation 3

Structured prompts are easier to improve over time.

# Prompt Engineering Principles

## Start With The Goal

Bad:

Write a post.

Better:

Create an educational Instagram post about hydration for the Beautery marketplace.

---

## Give Context

The more relevant context provided, the better the result.

Examples:

- Audience
- Platform
- Product category
- Desired outcome

---

## Define The Format

Specify:

- Instagram post
- Carousel
- Blog article
- Product description
- SEO article

---

## Define Constraints

Examples:

- No medical claims
- Simple language
- Friendly tone
- Practical recommendations

---

## Examples Improve Results

Provide examples whenever possible.

---

## Iteration Beats Perfection

The best prompts are refined over multiple versions.

---

## Concept Before Prompt

A strong idea usually matters more than a longer prompt.

---

## Output Structure Matters

Specify:

- Headline
- Introduction
- Main points
- Conclusion
- CTA

---

## Personal Lessons

### Lesson 1

Good context often matters more than prompt length.

### Lesson 2

Examples significantly improve output quality.

### Lesson 3

The first result is usually a draft, not the final version.

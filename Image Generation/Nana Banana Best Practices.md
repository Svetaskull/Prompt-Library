# Nana Banana Best Practices

## Purpose

Create realistic Instagram visuals for marketplace content.

---

# General Principles

## Focus On One Idea

Each image should communicate one clear concept.

Avoid:

- Multiple stories
- Overloaded scenes
- Too many focal points

---

## Photorealism First

Preferred style:

- Realistic photography
- Natural lighting
- Realistic materials
- Realistic skin
- Realistic products

Avoid:

- Cartoon style
- Illustration style
- Artificial-looking renders

---

# Instagram Requirements

## Format

Preferred:

- Vertical 4:5

Avoid:

- Square format

---

## Branding Space

Leave empty space at the top for logo placement.

---

# Product Photography

## Preferred Elements

- Transparent supplement jars
- Realistic capsules
- Realistic labels
- Premium appearance

---

## Environment

Preferred:

- Gray stone countertop
- Natural daylight
- Soft shadows
- Clean composition

---

## Hands

Preferred:

- Natural poses
- Nude manicure
- Minimal distractions

---

# Things To Avoid

## No Text

Do not generate:

- Headlines
- Captions
- Product descriptions

---

## No Infographics

Avoid:

- Charts
- Diagrams
- Labels
- Callouts

---

## No Before/After

Avoid comparison-style visuals.

---

## No Generic White Containers

Use realistic branded-looking packaging instead.

---

# Content Images

## Goal

Illustrate the topic without explaining it through text.

Examples:

- Summer fatigue
- Recovery
- Hydration
- Muscle cramps
- Wellness

---

# Lessons Learned

## Lesson 1

Simple scenes usually outperform complex scenes.

---

## Lesson 2

Realistic products increase perceived quality.

---

## Lesson 3

Natural lighting produces more trustworthy visuals.

---

## Lesson 4

A strong concept matters more than visual complexity.
